Smart Work Manager Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

This policy covers Smart Work Manager, its Web and Android application, and these product information pages operated by Vishal Creation. Contact: vishalmsk5@gmail.com. It does not replace separate policies for our other applications.

1. Information the application handles

  • Account and sign-in information, such as your email address and authentication session.
  • Work you enter: workspace, client, project and task details; notes; completion dates; report inputs; and uploaded evidence or content attachments.
  • Content and research inputs, including drafts, topics, keywords, hashtags, language selections and imported keyword reports.
  • When an optional external service is connected, the account identifiers, authorisation credentials and service data needed for the enabled feature.
  • Technical request information processed by hosting and backend services, such as IP addresses, request times and errors.

2. How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, save and display work, apply workspace permissions, calculate activity reports, handle uploads, support authorised integrations and diagnose failures. Reports reflect recorded data and may be incomplete when records are missing.

3. Google Ads access and keyword research

The Google Ads keyword integration is being configured. It is available only when the required Google approvals, account permissions and server configuration are in place.

When enabled, the integration uses an authorised Google Ads account and sends the selected keyword or topic, language and geographic targeting to Google. It retrieves keyword ideas and historical estimates such as average monthly searches and advertiser competition, and displays these results to signed-in application users who can access the configured research feature. These are Google Search estimates, not hashtag popularity or organic SEO difficulty.

OAuth access credentials and the configured Google Ads customer identifiers are handled on the backend for API requests. They must not be included in public website files, Android bundles or exported reports. The current research endpoint returns results for display rather than saving them as a keyword library. Results may remain in your application session or in files you choose to export. Authentication credentials remain in the configured backend until removed or replaced; access tokens also expire.

Google Ads data is used to provide the requested research feature. We do not sell Google user data, use it to train general-purpose AI models, or use it for unrelated advertising. We limit Google user-data use and sharing to providing or improving user-facing features, security, applicable legal obligations and other uses permitted by Google's API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Human access is limited to user-authorised support, security needs, legal requirements and other permitted cases.

You can revoke Google account authorisation through Google Account connections. Revocation prevents future authorised access once effective but does not erase reports you already exported. Contact us to request removal of stored connection credentials or retained application data.

4. Optional integrations, online research and AI

When you explicitly request public topic research, the selected query and language are sent to the configured public source, currently Wikipedia. Optional Instagram hashtag lookup sends the requested hashtag and required account authorisation to Meta. Connected social publishing, analytics or WordPress features exchange the content, account information and metrics needed for the action you request with that provider.

Local keyword analysis and editable writing templates do not require sending your text to a language model. If configured AI writing or planning is selected, your submitted prompt, draft and relevant editing context are sent to the operator-configured model service. Do not place passwords, customer secrets or unnecessary personal information in research queries or prompts.

5. Storage, sharing and service providers

Smart Work Manager uses Supabase for authentication, database and file storage. Hosting, backend and connected-platform providers process information needed to deliver their services and may process it in countries outside your own. Workspace members can see records shared with them according to application permissions; keep confidential material within appropriate private areas.

We do not sell your personal information. We share information with service providers to perform requested features, when you direct us to share or publish it, or when required by applicable law. Publishing to a social platform or WordPress makes the submitted content available according to that platform's visibility settings.

6. Retention and deletion

Saved application records are retained while needed to provide your workspace and until removed through supported controls or a verified deletion request. Deletion may depend on workspace ownership, other members' legitimate records and legal obligations. Provider logs and backups may remain under the relevant provider's retention procedures; we do not promise immediate removal from backups. We do not specify a fixed backup-erasure period.

To request account or data deletion, email vishalmsk5@gmail.com from your registered email address. Identify the account or workspace and the data concerned. Do not include passwords or tokens. We may verify identity and authority before acting. Copies you downloaded or published externally must also be managed with the relevant recipient or platform.

7. Security and your choices

Authentication, permission checks and restricted file storage are used to control access. No system can guarantee absolute security. Protect your account and devices, review workspace membership, and avoid uploading information you are not authorised to share. You can edit supported records, revoke external authorisation and contact us for access, correction or deletion requests.

8. Browser storage and voice input

The application uses browser or device storage to maintain sign-in sessions and preferences. These static product pages contain no analytics scripts, advertising scripts or cookies set by page code; the hosting provider may still process request logs. If you enable browser voice input or device dictation, audio processing depends on your browser, keyboard and operating-system provider and their settings.

9. Children and policy changes

Smart Work Manager is intended for work planning and is not directed to children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data that should be removed. We may update this policy as features or data practices change and will update the date above. Material changes will be communicated through available application or website channels as appropriate.
